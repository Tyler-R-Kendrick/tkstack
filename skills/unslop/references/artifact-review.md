# Review the artifact's purpose

Establish the brief, audience, invariant facts, and expected usage before judging
a pattern. A badge can convey real status; a comment can explain a hidden reason;
a familiar UI pattern may be the appropriate accessible affordance. Remove
ornament or repetition because of its concrete harm, not its supposed authorship.

For code, trace reachable behavior and readers' actual burden. For tests, ask
which wrong behavior the assertion detects and whether the expected value is
independent. Coverage or file counts alone do not establish useful checks.

For UI, inspect the rendered surface and relevant states at actual viewport
sizes. Evaluate hierarchy, task clarity, responsive behavior, keyboard use,
focus, contrast, loading/empty/error states, and motion preferences. Do not grade
visual craft from source or asset filenames alone.

For media, inspect the output rather than a successful generation response:
subject/reference fidelity, truthful text, anatomy or geometry where applicable,
lighting, continuity, cropping, composition, and the requested format. Verify
audio/video through appropriate playback or frame/waveform evidence. An image
thumbnail does not prove full-video continuity or sound quality.

Prioritize failures against purpose and objective quality floors, then confirmed
scoped preferences. Use current catalog exceptions. A source skill's taste is
an idea to consider, not a fabricated user correction or automatic active rule.
Keep missing inspection unreviewed and preserve read-only grading requests.
